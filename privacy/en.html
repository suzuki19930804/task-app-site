Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the iOS app "タスク" (Tasks; the "App") handles your information.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information or other data from you.

Where Your Data Is Stored

The tasks, to-dos, memos, tags, diary entries, settings and other data you create in the App are stored on your device. The App's widgets read and write this data through a shared area on the same device (an App Group). Your data is never sent to the developer's or any third party's servers.

Only if you turn on "Sync with iCloud" in Settings, your tasks, diary, tag colors and memo are stored in your own iCloud account and synced between your devices signed in with the same Apple ID. This data is stored on Apple's services and cannot be viewed by the developer. It is handled according to Apple's privacy policy.

Only if you choose a folder under "Folder Sync" in Settings, the App saves and reads your data as a single file (task-app-sync.json) in that folder. If the folder is in a cloud storage service such as iCloud Drive, Google Drive or OneDrive, the file is stored with that service under your own account and is handled according to that service's privacy policy. The developer cannot view this file.

"Export Data" in Settings saves your data as a single file in a location you choose. You are responsible for managing exported files.

Your data is deleted from your device when you delete the App. It may be included in device backups (such as iCloud Backup), which are handled according to Apple's privacy policy.

In-App Purchase

Purchases and restores of the Full Version are processed through Apple's App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple and is never received by the developer. The App stores only whether the Full Version has been purchased, on your device. When you use "Folder Sync" or "Export Data" on a device with the Full Version, the saved file includes a mark indicating the Full Version, so that the web version can enable the Full Version features.

Sharing With Third Parties

The App does not use any services or external libraries for advertising, analytics or tracking. We do not share your information with any third party.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us through the support page linked from the App's page on the App Store.

Changes

We may update this policy as needed. When we do, we will post the changes on this page along with the date of the last update.
